### ROW 3: Pakistan Tax (replaces "Backend and APIs")

Card: Income Tax for Salaried Individuals (Tax Year 2027)
Annual taxable income slabs:
* Up to Rs 600,000: 0%
* Rs 600,001 to Rs 1,200,000: 1% of the amount above Rs 600,000
* Rs 1,200,001 to Rs 2,200,000: Rs 6,000 plus 11% of the amount above Rs 1,200,000
* Rs 2,200,001 to Rs 3,200,000: Rs 116,000 plus 20% of the amount above Rs 2,200,000
* Rs 3,200,001 to Rs 4,100,000: Rs 316,000 plus 25% of the amount above Rs 3,200,000
* Rs 4,100,001 to Rs 5,600,000: Rs 541,000 plus 29% of the amount above Rs 4,100,000
* Rs 5,600,001 to Rs 7,000,000: Rs 976,000 plus 32% of the amount above Rs 5,600,000
* Above Rs 7,000,000: Rs 1,424,000 plus 35% of the amount above Rs 7,000,000
Highlight: The 9% surcharge on high salaried incomes was abolished from Tax Year 2027.

Card: Sales Tax in Pakistan
* Federal sales tax on goods: 18% (FBR)
* Sales tax on services: collected by provinces at roughly 13% to 16% (PRA, SRB, KPRA, BRA)
* Monthly sales tax returns are due in the following month
* E invoicing integration with FBR is becoming mandatory, and Finance Bill 2026 proposed a 10% tax credit for businesses that integrate with the real time reporting system

Card: Corporate Tax (Companies)
* Standard corporate tax rate: 29% for companies
* Super tax applies on high income companies (check the current slab each year)
* Minimum tax and advance tax apply in many cases

Card: Export of Services and Freelancers
* Export of services: 1% final tax deducted by the bank on remittance
* IT and IT enabled services registered with PSEB: reduced 0.25% rate, extension to Tax Year 2029 proposed in Finance Bill 2026 (confirm in the final Act)
* Freelancers should be on the Active Taxpayer List (ATL) and keep proper records

Card: Withholding Tax and Compliance
* Monthly withholding statements and deposit by the 15th of the following month
* Filer versus non filer rates differ a lot, so being on the Active Taxpayer List saves real money
* Finance Bill 2026 proposed a 5% withholding tax on revenue from social media platforms and a reduced 0.5% on foreign remittances via credit or debit cards (confirm final status)

Card: Online Sellers and E Commerce
* Online marketplaces and courier based sellers need income tax registration, and sales tax registration where applicable
* Payments for digitally ordered goods and services have specific tax treatment under the Finance Act 2025 rules

Return deadlines (usual): 30 September for individuals and AOPs, 31 December for companies. FBR often extends dates, so always check the latest notice.

### ROW 4: US Tax (replaces "Mobile Apps")

Card: Foreign Owned US LLC (Form 5472 and Pro Forma 1120)
* A single member LLC owned by a non US person is disregarded for income tax but still has reporting duties
* It files a pro forma Form 1120 with Form 5472 attached, even with zero revenue, if there is a reportable transaction with the owner (even a 1 dollar capital contribution)
* Due date: April 15 for calendar year entities
* Penalty for missing it: 25,000 dollars per form per year, plus 25,000 dollars for every 30 days after IRS notice
* Form 7004 extension filed before the due date extends the filing and helps avoid the penalty
* This filing is sent by paper or fax, not e file

Card: US Corporate Tax
* C Corporation federal tax rate: flat 21%
* Pass through entities (LLC, S Corp, partnership) pay tax at the owner level

Card: Nonresident and Freelancer US Forms
* Form W8BEN: lets non US individuals claim treaty benefits and avoid 30% default withholding
* Form 1040NR: nonresident individual return
* Form W9: for US persons and US entities
* Pakistan US tax treaty considerations for US sourced income

Card: US Individuals Living Abroad
* Form 1040 with Foreign Earned Income Exclusion (Form 2555) and Foreign Tax Credit (Form 1116)
* FBAR (FinCEN 114) for foreign accounts above 10,000 dollars combined at any time in the year
* Form 8938 (FATCA) when thresholds are met

Card: Pakistan and US Together
* Pakistani residents owning US LLCs must also declare foreign income and assets in Pakistan
* Correct structuring avoids double tax and penalties on both sides

## 7. FAQ SECTION

Q: Do I need to file a tax return if my income is below Rs 600,000?
A: Income up to Rs 600,000 is taxed at 0%, but filing a return and being on the Active Taxpayer List still brings big benefits, such as lower withholding rates on many transactions.

Q: I own a US LLC but live in Pakistan. What must I file?
A: Usually a pro forma Form 1120 with Form 5472 every year, even with no income. Missing it starts at a 25,000 dollar penalty.

Q: Is sales tax in Pakistan the same for goods and services?
A: No. Goods are taxed federally at 18%. Services are taxed by the provinces at different rates.

Q: Can you handle both Pakistan and US taxes?
A: Yes. I help with both sides so you stay compliant and avoid paying tax twice.
